    -D_CUDAX_ENABLE_GROUP_FEATURES_IN_LIBCUDACXX
    -D_CUB_WARPSPEED_USE_CUDAX_GROUPS
    -D_CUB_WARPSPEED_USE_STATIC_CUDAX_GROUPS

  resulting binary outside the sandbox. Full compiler commands, binaries,
