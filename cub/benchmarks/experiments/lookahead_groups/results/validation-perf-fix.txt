Compatibility limitation
  CUDA12 cannot target Blackwell or compile the PTX8.6 lookahead body. Its scan
  tests cover header/legacy compatibility. The separate actual group tests are
  instantiated as compute90 PTX and run through the driver's JIT on the RTX.
  Clang23 parses installed CUDA13.5 headers; its unknown-toolkit-version warning
  is disabled while repository checks remain enabled.
